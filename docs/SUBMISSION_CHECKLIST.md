# Submission checklist

Deadline: **18 November 2026, 12:00 PT = 19 November 2026, 01:30 IST.** Submit with hours to spare; Devpost slows down near deadlines.

## Eligibility (only you can do these)

- [ ] **Meta VR Start membership.** Apply at https://developers.meta.com/vr/discover/programs/start/ (about 5 minutes). Approval time is not published, so apply **today**. The person submitting must be a member *by the time of submission*.
- [ ] **Developer Access** enabled on your Meta account (developers.meta.com → sign in → create or verify your developer account).
- [ ] Confirm you are 18+ and resident in an eligible country. India is not excluded by the rules.
- [ ] Register on Devpost: the **Join hackathon** button on https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/. Accepting the rules is your decision; read them first.
