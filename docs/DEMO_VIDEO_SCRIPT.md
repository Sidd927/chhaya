# Demo video script

Target length is **2:45**; it must stay under 3:00. All footage is real application capture: either the Immersive Web Emulator (IWER) running the actual build, or a Quest 3 if one is available. No AI-generated video. If the footage comes from the emulator, say so on screen: *"Captured in Meta's Immersive Web Emulator."*

Tone: quiet and warm, like someone telling a story by lamplight. Speak slowly and leave room for the tanpura drone.

## Notes

- Keep the drone under the voice at about −18 dB. The app's own sound is synthesized, so record it or recreate it in the edit.
- Burn in captions for the narration (English).
- Show at least one moment of the real wall changing the experience: the reticle choosing a wall, and the stage landing on it.
- Don't claim performance numbers or device testing in the video unless they have been measured on a Quest.
