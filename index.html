A mixed-reality shadow theater for Meta Quest

Chhaya

A lantern throws your hands’ shadows onto your own wall. Hold a shape still, and it comes alive.

  • Hands only
  • Seated
  • About 8 minutes
  • Sound on

How the theater works

  1. 1Choose a wall. Look at it and pinch. No wall nearby? A paper screen appears instead.
  2. 2Raise a hand into the light. Your shadow falls on the wall, larger and softer the nearer you are to the lantern.
  3. 3Hold a shape still. It wakes up and walks away as a creature. Pinch its shadow to carry it.

Shadows to find

    Includes a retelling of The Lion and the Clever Hare from the Panchatantra. Works without controllers. Your progress stays on this headset.
